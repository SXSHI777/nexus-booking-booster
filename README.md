# Nexus Landing Page

Build a modern, single-page landing page for "Nexus Media Agency," an email marketing agency for coaches, consultants, and agency owners. Style: clean, dark navy background with a warm accent color, generous whitespace, bold headings, professional but approachable. Fully responsive.

Sections in order:

Sticky top bar with the name "Nexus Media Agency" and a "Book a call" button.

Hero: headline "Turn your email list into booked calls." with subhead "We build the email systems that welcome new leads, build trust, and get them on your calendar ready to buy. For coaches, consultants, and agency owners." Primary button "Book a call" linking to https://calendly.com/sufyaan-nexusmedia-agency/30min . Secondary link "Get a free email breakdown" scrolling to the contact form.

"Why leads go cold": three cards (leads sign up and go quiet; booked calls don't show up; emails sound generic).

"What we do": three cards (Email audit, Sequences written for you, Setup and optimization) with one-sentence descriptions.

"How it works": four numbered steps.

"Sample work": a side-by-side before/after email mockup, labeled clearly as an example.

"Who it's for": coaches, consultants, agency owners.

Final call to action with an embedded booking button and a simple contact form (name, email, website, message).

Footer with email "sufyaan@nexusmedia-agency.co.za" and a LinkedIn link https://www.linkedin.com/in/sufyaan-booley-639b8b347/.

Do not include testimonials, client logos, statistics, or awards. Use placeholder text only where I marked brackets. Add smooth scroll, subtle fade-in animations, and good page metadata for sharing.
